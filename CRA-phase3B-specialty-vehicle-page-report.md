# CRA-ENG Phase 3B Specialty Vehicle Page Report

Date: July 27, 2026

## Scope Completed

Created `specialty-vehicle-components.html` as the next substantive CRA-ENG market page.

The page positions CRA-ENG as a China Execution Partner for:

- Specialty vehicle manufacturers
- Vehicle upfitters
- Commercial vehicle conversion companies
- Mobile unit builders

The page explicitly states that CRA-ENG supports the companies that build and upfit specialty vehicles and is not a vehicle manufacturer, conversion company, or finished-vehicle supplier.

## Completed Page Structure

### 1. Hero

H1:

> Specialty Vehicle Components & Supply Chain Support

The hero identifies the target customers, defines CRA-ENG’s China-side component and supplier execution role, and provides direct links to project inquiry and capabilities.

### 2. Industry Challenges

Covered challenges:

- Low and medium production volumes
- Multiple suppliers and cross-component interfaces
- Custom application-specific components
- Prototype-to-production gaps
- Engineering communication across borders
- Change and service-supply continuity

A non-compete boundary follows the challenge cards to prevent visitors from interpreting CRA-ENG as a finished-vehicle company.

### 3. Components CRA-ENG Supports

Completed cards:

- Fabricated Assemblies
- Interior Components
- Electrical Systems
- Water and Fluid Systems
- Mounting Hardware
- Installation Kits

The component copy focuses on parts, fabricated assemblies, matched sets, replacement packages, and controlled repeat supply rather than complete vehicle design or manufacturing.

### 4. Supplier and Production Execution

Completed execution phases:

1. Supplier Qualification
2. Prototype Coordination
3. Production Follow-Up
4. Quality Control
5. Change Management

The section states that the vehicle builder retains product authority, system integration, compliance decisions, and final approval.

### 5. Vehicle Builder Responsibility Model

The page uses the approved three-party model:

- The client owns product requirements, architecture, compliance, integration, final approval, and product release.
- CRA-ENG owns agreed China-side supplier, prototype, readiness, quality, change, consolidation, and repeat-supply execution.
- The supplier owns manufacturing execution, conformance, production records, and supplier-caused corrective action.

### 6. Final CTA

The CTA asks qualified visitors to share the application, drawing or sample, supplier status, expected volume, development stage, and target timeline. It links to project inquiry and confidentiality information.
