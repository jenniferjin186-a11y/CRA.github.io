Beyond supplier selection

Why Factory Sourcing Is Not Enough

A capable supplier is essential, but supplier selection does not manage technical interpretation, prototype control, production readiness, quality evidence, engineering changes, or coordination across multiple factories.

Direct factory management can work when requirements are stable, internal resources are available in China, and one supplier owns the complete scope. Specialty mobile-unit programs often have the opposite conditions: application-specific parts, multiple interfaces, evolving details, specialized volumes, and little tolerance for late surprises.

Common execution gaps

Where China Programs Lose Control

The gap is rarely just finding a factory. It is maintaining shared understanding and disciplined follow-through from requirement to repeat order.

Communication Gaps

Questions move between commercial and technical teams without the full installation, service, or operating context.

Supplier Misunderstanding

A drawing or sample may not capture interfaces, acceptable substitutions, critical dimensions, or the reason behind a requirement.

Prototype-to-Production Drift

Approved sample materials, purchased parts, finishes, or processes may change when production begins.

Late Quality Discovery

Issues found after shipment create expensive containment, rework, schedule, and customer-service problems.

Uncontrolled Changes

Revisions, deviations, and substitutions can be implemented without clear approval or consistent supplier records.

Fragmented Supply

Several acceptable components can still fail to form a compatible, complete, and serviceable installation kit.

Clear ownership

Client, CRA-ENG, and Supplier Responsibilities

The client retains product authority. CRA-ENG owns agreed China-side execution. Each supplier remains responsible for its manufacturing process and conformance.

The Client Owns

  • Product strategy and requirements
  • Vehicle and system architecture
  • Compliance decisions
  • Final approval, integration, and release

CRA-ENG Owns Within Scope

  • China-side technical communication
  • Supplier and prototype coordination
  • First article, quality, and change control
  • Consolidation and production follow-through

The Supplier Owns

  • Manufacturing-process execution
  • Conformance to approved requirements
  • Agreed process and quality records
  • Supplier-caused corrective action
