Industry challenges

Mobile Equipment Programs Need Consistent Execution Across Specialized Supply

Field service, utility, and commercial equipment programs often combine custom low-volume parts, multiple suppliers, prototype development, demanding field conditions, and technical communication across time zones.

Custom Low-Volume Components

Programs need suppliers capable of application-specific parts, practical batch quantities, variants, replacement supply, and repeat orders.

Multiple Supplier Coordination

Fabricated, electrical, mechanical, fluid, control, and mounting components must remain compatible and arrive ready for equipment integration.

Prototype Development

Samples require clear requirements, issue tracking, correction follow-up, and documented decisions before production release.

Manufacturing Consistency

Approved materials, purchased parts, processes, finishes, tolerances, and packaging must remain controlled from first article through repeat production.

Field-Use Reliability

Components operate in mobile working environments where vibration, weather exposure, service access, duty cycle, and installation consistency matter.

Supplier Communication

Technical questions, deviations, substitutions, quality issues, and changes require local follow-through and clear client approval points.

CRA-ENG supports the companies that build mobile equipment. We are not a finished-equipment manufacturer, factory, generic sourcing agent, or product reseller.

Component scope

Components CRA-ENG Supports

The scope remains focused on custom components, fabricated assemblies, supplier execution, production control, and repeat supply—not complete equipment manufacturing.

Custom Fabricated Assemblies

Frames, brackets, panels, welded assemblies, formed parts, machined parts, molded components, and application-specific subassemblies.

Electrical Systems

Auxiliary power components, harnesses, electrical enclosures, protection components, interface hardware, and coordinated electrical subassemblies.

Enclosures and Mounting Structures

Equipment enclosures, cabinets, supports, mounting frames, access panels, protective structures, and model-specific interface hardware.

Fluid and Mechanical Systems

Pumps, valves, fittings, hoses, tanks, mechanical linkages, motion components, hardware, and related fabricated or purchased assemblies.

Control Components

Operator controls, switches, control boxes, display mounts, interface panels, wiring provisions, and coordinated control subassemblies.

Installation Kits

Matched component sets, labeled hardware, model-specific packing, replacement packages, and repeatable supply organized for equipment installation.

Supplier and production execution

China-Side Control From Supplier Qualification Through Repeat Production

CRA-ENG manages agreed execution activities while the client retains product requirements, system design, integration decisions, and final approval.

  1. Supplier Qualification

    Assess process capability, quality discipline, communication, capacity, volume fit, and application-specific risk.

  2. Prototype Coordination

    Clarify requirements, manage samples, track issues, coordinate corrections, and prepare first-article decisions.

  3. Production Follow-Up

    Confirm readiness, approved inputs, production status, packaging, consolidation needs, and repeat-order requirements.

  4. Quality Control

    Follow agreed inspections, evidence, nonconformances, corrective actions, and supplier quality commitments.

  5. Change Management

    Keep revisions, substitutions, deviations, and implementation status visible across affected suppliers and components.

Responsibility model

The Client Keeps Product Authority

CRA-ENG extends the client’s China-side execution capability without becoming the product company, finished-equipment manufacturer, or sales channel.

The Client Owns

  • Product requirements and commercial direction
  • System design and equipment architecture
  • Compliance and integration decisions
  • Final component approval and product release

CRA-ENG Owns Within Scope

  • China-side execution
  • Supplier and technical coordination
  • Prototype follow-up and production readiness
  • Quality control, change follow-up, and repeat supply

The Supplier Owns

  • Manufacturing-process execution
  • Production conformity to approved requirements
  • Agreed manufacturing and quality records
  • Supplier-caused corrective action
