# CRA-ENG Phase 1 Website Content Plan

## Strategic Foundation

### Primary target customer

CRA-ENG’s primary target customer is an established company similar to Wag’n Tails that designs, manufactures, converts, or upfits specialized mobile units.

Priority audiences include:

- Mobile grooming vehicle manufacturers
- Mobile grooming unit builders
- Grooming van conversion companies
- Mobile veterinary and pet service vehicle builders
- Specialty vehicle manufacturers
- Commercial vehicle upfitters
- Equipment brands supplying specialized mobile units

### Positioning

> CRA-ENG is the China Execution Partner for specialty vehicle manufacturers and mobile equipment companies—managing custom components, suppliers, prototypes, production, quality, consolidation, and delivery.

### Positioning boundary

CRA-ENG is not:

- A pet product company
- A mobile grooming service
- A seller of finished grooming vans
- A general consumer vehicle brand
- A supplier directory
- A transactional sourcing agent
- A finished specialty vehicle manufacturer

CRA-ENG supports the companies that build specialized mobile units. The client retains ownership of the vehicle platform, system architecture, regulatory decisions, final integration, and customer experience. CRA-ENG manages agreed China-side component and manufacturing execution.

### Primary business promise

> CRA-ENG gives specialty vehicle manufacturers an accountable team in China to turn component requirements into reliable production supply.

### Core terminology

Use:

- Mobile grooming vehicle manufacturer
- Mobile grooming unit builder
- Grooming van conversion company
- Specialty vehicle manufacturer
- Vehicle upfitter
- Specialized mobile unit
- Custom component
- Fabricated assembly
- Vehicle installation kit
- Supplier development
- First article
- Production readiness
- Quality control
- Change control
- Multi-supplier consolidation
- Repeat production supply

Avoid:

- Pet products
- Pet accessories
- Grooming van dealer
- Cheap sourcing
- Best price from China
- One-stop shop for everything
- We manufacture any product
- Finished grooming vans from China

- Vehicle upfitters
