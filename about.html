Who we are

China-Based Support for Cross-Border Manufacturing Execution

CRA-ENG extends the China-side capability of companies that own and build specialized vehicles and mobile equipment.

Our role combines engineering and manufacturing understanding with supplier communication and cross-border project coordination. We help client teams turn approved requirements into supplier actions, prototype decisions, production-readiness evidence, quality follow-up, and controlled repeat supply.

CRA-ENG is supplier-independent and execution-focused. We do not present ourselves as a factory, manufacture complete vehicles, or replace the client’s product and engineering authority.

What makes CRA-ENG different

Execution Requires More Than a Supplier Introduction

Effective China-side support depends on understanding the requirement, communicating it accurately, following the manufacturing work, and making issues visible before they become shipment problems.

Engineering Understanding

We work from application context, interfaces, drawings, samples, critical features, and acceptance criteria—not only commercial descriptions.

Supplier Communication

We coordinate technical questions, deviations, corrections, decisions, and follow-up between client and supplier teams.

Manufacturing Execution

We follow supplier qualification, prototypes, first articles, readiness, production activity, and repeat-order requirements within scope.

Quality Control Mindset

We focus on defined requirements, practical evidence, issue tracking, corrective action, and controlled changes rather than late-stage inspection alone.

Our role

Clear Responsibility Across the Program

The client owns the product and customer experience. CRA-ENG owns agreed China-side execution. The supplier owns its manufacturing process and conformance.

The Client Owns

  • Product direction and commercial priorities
  • Specifications and system architecture
  • Compliance decisions
  • Final approval and product release

CRA-ENG Owns Within Scope

  • China-side execution
  • Supplier coordination and communication
  • Prototype and first-article follow-up
  • Production readiness, quality, and change follow-up

The Supplier Owns

  • Manufacturing-process execution
  • Conformance to approved requirements
  • Agreed records and production controls
  • Supplier-caused corrective action

Why companies work with CRA-ENG

Consistent Follow-Through
