# CRA-ENG Phase 3A Trust Pages Completion Report

Date: July 27, 2026

## Phase 3A Scope

Phase 3A adds three trust-building pages without changing the approved CRA-ENG visual direction. The pages reuse the existing Phase 2 interior-page system: navy and blue colors, full-width section backgrounds, centered section headings, restrained cards, established CTA treatments, shared spacing, responsive containers, and the existing header/footer loading structure.

No redesign, new imagery, decorative marketing treatment, unsupported company statistics, factory claims, customer names, or invented performance results were introduced.

## Completed Pages

### About CRA-ENG

File: `about.html`

Completed sections:

1. Hero
2. Who We Are
3. What Makes CRA-ENG Different
4. Client / CRA-ENG / Supplier Responsibility Model
5. Why Companies Work With CRA-ENG
6. Final CTA

Key trust decisions:

- Positions CRA-ENG as a China-based, engineering-led execution partner rather than a traditional consultant or factory.
- Explains engineering and manufacturing understanding, supplier communication, cross-border coordination, production follow-up, and quality-control discipline.
- States that CRA-ENG is supplier-independent and does not manufacture complete vehicles or replace client product authority.
- Uses the approved ownership model: the client owns product direction and approval; CRA-ENG owns agreed China-side execution; the supplier owns manufacturing execution and conformance.
- Explains the commercial need for local China capability without requiring the client to build its own China office.

### Confidentiality and Project Protection

File: `nda-confidentiality.html`

Completed sections:

1. Hero
2. Why Confidentiality Matters
3. CRA-ENG Confidentiality Approach
4. Working Under NDA
5. Final CTA

Key trust decisions:

- Identifies drawings, product specifications, prototype information, and supplier requirements as typical confidential project inputs.
- Describes controlled communication, document context, supplier-information handling, and prototype confidentiality as working practices.
- States that CRA-ENG can review and work under NDA requirements before detailed technical discussion or supplier engagement.
- Includes an explicit boundary that the page describes a working approach and is not legal advice or a substitute for a project-specific agreement.
- Avoids guarantees, legal conclusions, and law-firm positioning.
