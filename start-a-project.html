Project inquiry

Start With the Information You Have

A drawing package is not required for the first discussion. A component description, supplier quotation, physical sample, prototype issue, quality finding, or repeat-supply problem can provide a useful starting point.

Useful context to include

  • Your company and the product or equipment you build
  • The component, assembly, or supplier challenge
  • Current development or production stage
  • Available drawings, samples, or inspection evidence
  • Expected annual or batch volume, if known
  • Required decision or delivery timeline

Good project fit

When CRA-ENG Is Most Relevant

The strongest fit is a manufacturer or equipment brand that owns its product and needs accountable China-side execution across one or more suppliers.

Custom Component Program

The part or assembly must meet application-specific requirements rather than simply match a catalog specification.

Execution Gap in China

The internal team needs stronger technical communication, supplier follow-up, validation, quality, or change control.

Repeat Supply Requirement

The program needs controlled production, consolidation, kits, replacements, or recurring orders—not only a supplier introduction.
