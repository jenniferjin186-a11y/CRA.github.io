Why confidentiality matters

Execution Requires Access to Sensitive Product Information

Specialty vehicle and equipment programs often require enough detail for suppliers to quote, prototype, validate, manufacture, and correct custom components.

Drawings

Dimensions, tolerances, interfaces, materials, finishes, revisions, and other product-defining information.

Product Specifications

Performance requirements, operating conditions, installation constraints, acceptance criteria, and service expectations.

Prototype Information

Physical samples, test findings, issue lists, correction details, photographs, and approval status.

Supplier Requirements

Quotations, process expectations, quality controls, approved sources, production records, and commercial context.

CRA-ENG’s approach is to share information according to the agreed execution need, maintain clear document context, and avoid unnecessary disclosure outside the project scope.

Confidentiality approach

Practical Controls Around Project Communication

The exact controls depend on the client’s requirements, the agreed NDA, the supplier structure, and the information needed for each execution activity.

Controlled Communication

Project information is communicated to the relevant participants for defined quotation, technical, prototype, quality, or production activities.

Documentation Management

Drawings, specifications, samples, revisions, deviations, and approvals are kept in context so suppliers act from the current agreed information.

Supplier Information Handling

Supplier identities, quotations, capability information, quality findings, and project records are handled within the client relationship and agreed scope.

Prototype Confidentiality

Prototype descriptions, images, samples, issue findings, and evaluation details are treated as project information and are not presented publicly without approval.

Working under NDA

Confidentiality Requirements Can Be Agreed Before Technical Disclosure

CRA-ENG can review and work under NDA requirements before detailed technical discussions, supplier engagement, drawing exchange, or prototype coordination begins.

Before Detailed Review

An initial conversation can stay at a high level while the parties determine fit and identify the confidentiality framework needed for deeper evaluation.

Before Supplier Engagement

The information required for supplier quotation or technical review can be discussed with the client before it is shared into the supply chain.

During the Program

New drawings, revisions, supplier questions, prototype findings, and production records can be managed according to the agreed project process.

Important: this page describes CRA-ENG’s working approach and is not legal advice or a substitute for a project-specific agreement reviewed by the appropriate parties.
